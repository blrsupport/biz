## Where the film departs from the first board (the user was told)

- The sun is at frame left, so the buyer comes in from the left and the shadow lies to the right.
- The year goes behind a stack of drums that passes close to the lens, not behind a heap.
- The piece is thrown by an arm that comes up from below the frame; no second full figure.
- "take a company public" is written across the sky like the other type, not on the wall.
