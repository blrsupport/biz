# Bihar Mushroom: board v2 (scenes rewritten after rough cut 2)

110.33 s of tightened voice. 4 worlds, 7 sequences, no cuts inside a world. Written 2 Oct 2026 after the user scored rough cut 2 at 2/10 ("arms twisting, robots, the first scene is so bad"). Board v1 is in `board_v1.md`. Nothing is built from this yet: the build waits for next week.

## The idea of the film in one line

A white sack goes **from Haryana to Bihar** in the first shot and **from Bihar to Haryana** in the last, at the same lorry, handed over by the same trader. Everything in between is how that reversal happened: a degree turned down, a road to the hills, six years of grey bags, a lab, and a table where farmers learn by copying.

## Staging rules for this film (from what went wrong in cut 2)

1. **Nothing is picked up off the floor.** Props live at waist height: the lorry's tailboard, a crate, a bench, a rack shelf, the top of a straw stack. No deep crouches.
2. **Hand-overs happen close.** Two people stand no more than one head apart, the thing passes at chest height, and both hands hold it. No long reaches, no reaching across the body with the far arm.
3. **Nobody turns round while holding something.** Turn first, then take it; or put it down, turn, take it up again.
4. **One person acts at a time.** The others react with eyes, head and face only. No group nodding in turn.
5. **Two people beside Sanjeev at most** in any frame. Crowds are far, small, and pass through the background.
6. **Shadows instead of black figures.** The gossips, the doubting farmer and the porter are long shadows thrown through the doorway onto the floor and wall. Farmers who come inside are in full colour (BUYER and PORTER re-dressed), never black blobs.
7. **Small actions get close-ups.** Coins in a tin, tearing a bag, white threads in a bottle, a knot being tied: the camera goes to the hands.
8. **Something changes in every beat.** No hold longer than 1.25 s; no standing and looking for more than a second.
9. **Every beat is checked as a picture before it is rendered.** One preview sheet per sequence, looked at by the main session, before the half-size render.
