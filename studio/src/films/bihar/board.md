# Bihar Mushroom: board

Written from `node tools/script.ts bihar` (110.33 s tightened). Film in one sentence: an engineer who chose his village fixed the one thing that held Bihar's mushroom farmers back, the seed, and now Bihar sells it to the state it used to buy from.

## Lite limits this board keeps

- 2 worlds (village outside; the mushroom shed that becomes the lab), 6 sequences, 5 planned joins.
- 1 new set (the village). The shed is the library room (`room.ts`) re-dressed in mud and thatch.
- New props (6): grow bag (hanging straw cylinder that sprouts oyster mushrooms), spawn bottle (grain with white threads), spawn sack (from `scale.ts` sack, re-dressed white), rack of spawn bottles, tin trunk, rolled degree.
- New people (3): Sanjeev young, Sanjeev today (same head, older), his father. The Haryana trader is IRAKI and the Bihar farmer is BUYER, re-dressed. Villagers, gossips, trainees are silhouettes (PORTER, SON_A, SON_B).
- Simplified to fit: Solan is not its own place; it is blue pine hills at the far left end of the village's skyline. No trucks or maps: sacks are carried by hand. "Thousands of small families" is a pull-back over many small huts with bags, with a few silhouettes. Nobody speaks on screen.
